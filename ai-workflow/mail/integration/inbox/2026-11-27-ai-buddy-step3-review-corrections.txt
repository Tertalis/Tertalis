REQUEST / INFORMATION
S3-R1 (nested Job eligibility published early) - FIXED.
  - The thing deferred is now eligibility itself, not only the scheduling of
    callbacks that were already collected.
  - When Job.endExecution() runs while a jobDispatchBoundary is open on the
    thread, the Job stays not-eligible and is queued as a deferred
    eligibility entry. The runJob wrapper's own boundary is always open at
    that point.
  - Until the outermost boundary exits, a registration from any thread is
    queued. At the outermost exit (_flush), the Job becomes eligible and
    every queued callback runs once.
  - An unowned Job keeps standalone semantics: it is eligible at terminal
    publication, and wait() is unchanged. Only dispatch on the current
    thread is deferred while that thread is inside a boundary.

S3-R2 (callback BaseException loses registrations or replaces the primary) -
FIXED.
  - _invokeCallbacks treats any BaseException raised by a callback as a
    notification failure. It is reported, the remaining callbacks still run,
    and the Job is never changed.
  - A reporter failure, including a BaseException, is logged and cannot
    stop dispatch.
  - _flush handles every pending entry before any propagation.
  - After all callbacks have run, critical (non-Exception) failures are
    handled as follows:
    - If a primary exception is propagating through the boundary, the
      primary is kept and receives a note per critical failure.
    - Otherwise the first critical failure propagates, with notes for any
      others.
  - Real capability interrupts are untouched: they are the primary.

S3-R3 (direct invokeCapability and lifecycle paths dispatch under locks) -
FIXED.
  - NEW decorator dispatchBoundary (backend/orchestration/runtime.py v12).
    It is applied outermost, above every lane and decorated boundary, on
    every public entry that runs Pack code:
    - ApplicationRuntime (v30): invokeCapability, stop, close;
    - PackLoader (backend/packs/runtime.py v20): activate, activatePack,
      close, invokeApplicationCreate, invokeApplicationLoad,
      invokeApplicationRun;
    - ApplicationLifecycle (backend/application/lifecycle.py v9): create,
      load, resume, abort;
    - RuntimeHost (backend/runtime/runtimeHost.py v16): createApplication,
      loadApplication, closeApplicationRun, stop.
    Inner entries nest, so dispatch happens at the outermost one. For
    example, onUnload under ApplicationRuntime.close dispatches after
    close leaves the runtime lane, not at the inner PackLoader.close.
  - tests/backend/application/test_compositionAdmission.py (v2) unwraps one
    more level, because invokeCapability gained the outer wrapper.

Non-blocking notes:
  - Job.start() intentionally keeps its preliminary "only Pending" check.
    It then delegates to tryStart(), so a cancel in between gives Cancelled,
    as before.
  - The ManualClock advance-under-condition caution is recorded for step 4.

Tests: tests/backend/orchestration/test_jobCompletion.py v2, 32 tests
(21 + 11 new):
  R1:
    - A nested Job is not eligible until its outer boundary exits. Another
      thread registers while the outer capability is gated after the nested
      runJob returned. Covered with the outer as runJob and as direct
      invokeCapability. The callback runs exactly once on the runner thread
      with the runtime lane and the Job lock acquirable from a third thread.
    - Standalone publication inside a boundary still makes the Job eligible
      for another thread.
  R2:
    - A critical callback followed by an ordinary one (both run, then the
      interrupt propagates, Job unchanged).
    - A critical callback inside a boundary does not drop another Job's
      pending entry.
    - Reporter failure, both Exception and KeyboardInterrupt.
    - Exceptional runJob exit: the primary interrupt is kept with a note,
      and all callbacks run after lock release.
    - A critical callback after a normal runJob propagates after all
      callbacks.
  R3:
    - Direct invokeCapability: registration on an eligible Job and
      cancellation of a Pending Job are deferred to the invocation exit, with
      locks released.
    - A nested Job inside a direct invocation is deferred.
    - Pack onLoad (PackLoader.activate) and onUnload (ApplicationRuntime.
      close) registrations dispatch after the call returns, with the runtime
