REQUEST / INFORMATION
Disposition per finding:

G8-R1 (cleanup-only failure reported resolved) - FIXED.
  Cleanup evidence is now execution-owned, not read from the primary.
  NEW in backend/orchestration/runtime.py (v10):
    JobCleanupEvidence: a per-Job collector.
    cleanupEvidenceScope: a context-variable scope.
    recordCleanupFailures.
    InvocationCleanupFailed.
  runJob opens one JobCleanupEvidence scope around everything after
  admission. CodeEntryContext.invalidate (v33) reports every cleanup error
  to the current Job's collector first. It does this on every path: with a
  primary exception, on the ExceptionGroup raise, and on the critical-only
  raise.
  _terminateJob classifies only from that evidence plus its own IO/unit
  cleanup errors. A capability's own ExceptionGroup therefore stays an
  ordinary failure with resolved authority.

G8-R2 (nested evidence overwritten/lost) - FIXED.
  Every nested invocation in the Job reports to the same collector, so
  nothing is overwritten.
  If the capability returns normally but the collector is non-empty (it
  caught a nested failure whose cleanup failed), the staging is not
  committed. The Job fails with InvocationCleanupFailed and authority
  unresolved, and JobCleanupFailed is raised.
  The primary's invocationCleanupErrors attribute is kept for compatibility
  but now accumulates instead of being overwritten.

G8-R3 (unsafe later admission) - FIXED, run-scoped and independent of any
executor.
  A Job ending unresolved or partially-accepted records itself as
  ApplicationRuntime.unsafeAuthority. After that, ApplicationRunUnsafe
  (NEW, backend/application/applicationRuntime.py v27) refuses:
    - runJob, before any Job transition, unit, transaction or handler (a
      supplied Pending Job stays Pending);
    - every invokeCapability whose memory view is not a Job transaction,
      i.e. anything that could reach authoritative state directly;
    - an outer Job's commit, when a nested Job made the run unsafe while
      it ran.
  There is no reset and no automatic recovery. close/stop are unchanged,
  and existing quarantine is untouched.
  Inspection that remains: unsafeAuthority (the culprit terminal Job, with
  its error, cleanupErrors and authority), lastJob, and host-side reads of
  committed state. Read capabilities invoked at top level are refused,
  consistent with CP-8.03.

G8-R4 (boundary / early failures) - FIXED.
  runJob notes the admitted Job on its LLM mutation scope (new
  noteJobMutation in backend/llm/executionSessions.py v10).
  jobExecutionBoundary now resolves exceptional exits from that Job's real
  authoritativeStateAccepted. This covers interrupts and JobCleanupFailed,
  and replaces the earlier JobCleanupFailed-only special case.
  NEW ApplicationRuntime.lastJob gives the caller terminal evidence for
  internally created Jobs when an interrupt propagates.

Tests: tests/backend/application/test_openTransactionFailure.py v2, 21
tests (7 original + 14 new):
  R1: cleanup-only failure is unresolved and raises the typed error;
      several cleanup-only errors are all kept; a critical cleanup interrupt

  R2: nested inner and outer distinct cleanup failures are both kept; a

  R3: after partial acceptance (IO commit restored) and after unresolved,

  R4: IO open failure before unit start; unit start failure; independent IO

      acceptance (scope True, partially-accepted, run unsafe); ordinary
      success/failure scope compatibility.
  Mutation checks: removing the evidence report fails 8 tests, removing
