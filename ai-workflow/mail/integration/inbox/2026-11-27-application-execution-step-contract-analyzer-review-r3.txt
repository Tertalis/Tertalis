REQUEST / INFORMATION
Recommend proceeding through normal staged implementation authorization and
independent acceptance. The design is sufficient to begin; design review is
not implementation acceptance or a grant of worker/branch authority.
G8 is the first independent slice. Preserve scope and exact accepted SHA
dependencies for subsequent stages.

Implementation acceptance attention (not a request for another design
revision):
- G8: retain primary and cleanup evidence, distinguish resolved authority
  from partial/unresolved acceptance, and verify no unsafe subsequent
  caller-driven execution is admitted. Cover failures outside the ordinary
  capability exception path; terminal guarantee applies to admitted Jobs.
- Job completion: prove ownership/dispatch eligibility across pending
  cancellation, registration races and exceptional exit. Callback dispatch
  must occur after scope release and coherent executor outcome/control
  publication, not just after an inner runJob returns. Ensure one actual
  tryStart transition, not one in each layer. Test callbacks requesting
  termination from the worker itself: no self-join or premature cleanup.
- Executor: prove exact admission authority, no lost wake, no step on clock
  notification alone, cancellation opportunity, unsafe queue disposition,
  and read-only platform-mediated inspection including nested calls.
- Lifecycle: shared termination ownership must preserve existing semantic
  distinction between stop and close (and hosted unregistration authority),
  not accidentally collapse them for legacy callers. Check pending
  initialization as well as published runtimes during host shutdown.
  Incomplete executor or resource cleanup must remain honestly retryable;
  no self-wait, duplicate cleanup, or resource destruction while live work
  owns it. These are implementation acceptance checks under existing
  lifecycle and compatibility requirements, not new feature scope.
- AI Buddy: verify host notification/delivery including initial pending
  outbox on restart, ack failure, interruption, and breakpoint semantics;
  preserve at-least-once delivery contract and explicit platform adoption.

Review recommendation: no further speculative design iteration required.
Move remaining verification to concrete code, deterministic regressions
and independent acceptance at each gate. Do not pre-authorize later
implementation acceptance based solely on this document review.
