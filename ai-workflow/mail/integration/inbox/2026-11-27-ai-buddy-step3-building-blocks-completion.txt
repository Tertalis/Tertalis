REQUEST / INFORMATION
1. Job completion (CP-10), in backend/orchestration/runtime.py (v11).

   New Job methods:
     - Job.wait(timeout=None) -> bool. It uses a Condition on the Job lock,
       which is notified at terminal publication.
     - Job.addDoneCallback(callback). callback(job) runs exactly once per
       registration.
     - Job.beginExecution(failureReporter=None) -> bool. On an already
       terminal Job it returns False and takes no ownership.
     - Job.endExecution().
     - Job.tryStart() -> bool. In one Job-locked step it starts a Pending
       Job, or turns a cancellation-requested Pending Job into Cancelled,
       or reports an already Cancelled one. It raises for any other state.
       start() is kept and now delegates to tryStart.

   Eligibility follows the three moments:
     - Terminal publication wakes waiters.
     - A Job with no owner becomes eligible at that publication.
     - An owned Job becomes eligible only at endExecution().
   Registering on an eligible Job dispatches at once. Registering between
   publication and eligibility queues the callback.

   NEW jobDispatchBoundary():
     - A per-thread depth counter and deferred list.
     - Callbacks that become due while any boundary is open on the thread
       (registration, cancelling a Pending Job, endExecution) are queued.
     - The queue is flushed at the outermost exit, normally or by
       exception.
     - Outside any boundary, callbacks run on the calling thread, after the
       Job lock is released.

   Failure isolation:
     - A failing callback (Exception) never changes the Job and never stops
       the other callbacks.
     - The failure goes to the owner's failureReporter, or else to the
       logger "tertalis.orchestration.jobs".
     - BaseException from a callback is not swallowed.

2. runJob as the execution owner, in backend/application/applicationRuntime.py
   (v29).
   - Public runJob is now an undecorated outermost wrapper. It opens
     jobDispatchBoundary, takes ownership with beginExecution(failureReporter
     = trace "job-done-callback-failed"), calls the decorated _runJob, and in
     a finally block calls endExecution().
   - Dispatch therefore happens after every runtime lane, lock and decorated
     boundary is left, on normal and exceptional exit alike.
   - _runJob uses tryStart() instead of check-then-start. A refused request
     (unsafe run, closed runtime) releases ownership and leaves a supplied
     Job Pending.
   - invokeCapability is not a dispatch boundary. CP-10.03 names admitted
     Jobs; step 4 will add the executor's own boundary.

3. Clock (CP-11).
   NEW backend/runtime/clock.py (v1):
     - Clock (ABC): now() returns an aware local datetime, monotonic()
       returns seconds, plus generation and
       waitFor(condition, predicate, deadline=monotonic seconds).
     - SystemClock: waits in slices of at most 60 s
       (WALL_RECONCILE_SECONDS), re-checking the predicate.
     - ManualClock(start, monotonicStart=0.0): advance(seconds) moves wall
       and monotonic together; setWall() moves wall only. Both bump the
       generation and notify every condition currently in waitFor. The
       generation check closes the window between a waiter's check and its
       wait.
     - requireAware rejects naive datetimes.
   RuntimeHost (v15) gains clock= and passes its single clock to every
   ApplicationRuntime (v29, clock=, default SystemClock).
   CodeEntryContext (v34) exposes read-only ctx.clock.now() and
   ctx.clock.monotonic(). They become invalid with the context. Payload
   "now" keeps working for migration.

4. Manifest declaration (CP-2.01), in backend/packs/runtime.py (v19).

   - backend/capabilities/runtime.py (v5) exports requireCapabilityId for
