SHARED PLATFORM CHANGE NOTICE (when applicable)
------------------------------------------------
Use the existing FROM/TO/SUBJECT/STATUS/CONTEXT fields above. An
accepted shared-platform change with possible Application impact should
also state, either here or in a single linked knowledge record:

ACCEPTED TERTALIS SHA
<exact commit for the accepted platform implementation>

CHANGE / CONTRACT IMPACT
<changed capability, interface, behavior, requirements, guarantees;
 distinguish verified facts from potential effects>

POTENTIALLY AFFECTED CONSUMER
<this recipient and why it might be affected, or uncertainty>

ADAPTATION / INVESTIGATION
<what the Application implementer/Integrator should examine or change;
 explicitly state UNKNOWN when impact is not established>

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
<tests/evidence at exact refs, untested boundaries>

AUTHORITY
<notification only: no Application platform SHA changes or automatic
 synchronization unless a separately authorized Integrator accepts it>

When notifying multiple consumers, send recipient-specific addressed
messages and link shared evidence rather than copying a mutable
application-location registry or separate authoritative state.
